### 2. Universal Strategy Architecture
We partition the search space into two deterministic sweeps. Let $t$ be the time step and $S_t$ be the shot location:
* **Phase 1 (Forward Sweep):** Shoot $2, 3, \dots, n-1$. 
  At $t=1$, we shoot $S_1 = 2$. If the target started on an EVEN spot, its position parity matches our shot parity ($P_t \equiv S_t \pmod 2$). Because we sweep monotonically, the target cannot cross our line of fire without being hit.
* **Phase 2 (Backward Sweep):** Shoot $n-1, n-2, \dots, 2$. 
  If Phase 1 fails, the target started on an ODD spot. Phase 1 ends at $t = n-2$. Our next shot is at $t = n-1$. 
  By shooting at $S_{n-1} = n-1$, we force a mathematical parity synchronization:
  $$P_{n-1} \equiv P_1 + (n-1) - 1 \equiv 1 + n - 2 \equiv n - 1 \pmod 2$$
  Since $S_{n-1} \equiv n-1 \pmod 2$, the parities align perfectly for *both* odd and even $n$. Sweeping backwards guarantees interception.
