# Q5 — Candy Distribution Problem

**File:** `q5.c`

## Problem

`n` children stand in a line, each with a rating. Every child must receive at least one candy. If a child's rating is higher than an adjacent child's rating, that child must receive strictly more candies than the neighbour. Find the minimum total number of candies and the count per child.

## Input

- `n` — number of children
- `n` ratings from left to right

## Output

- Candy count per child
- Minimum total candies

## Approach

Do two independent passes and take the maximum at each position.

1. **Left-to-right pass** — walk the line once, ensuring every child with a higher rating than the left neighbour gets one more candy than that neighbour.
2. **Right-to-left pass** — same idea but comparing with the right neighbour.
3. `candies[i] = max(left[i], right[i])`

Each pass alone is a lower bound on the required count; the max combines both constraints.

$$
\text{left}[i] = \begin{cases}
\text{left}[i-1] + 1 & \text{if } \text{rating}[i] > \text{rating}[i-1] \\
1 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\text{right}[i] = \begin{cases}
\text{right}[i+1] + 1 & \text{if } \text{rating}[i] > \text{rating}[i+1] \\
1 & \text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
$$

$$
\text{candies}[i] = \max\bigl(\text{left}[i],\ \text{right}[i]\bigr)
$$

## Complexity

- **Time:** O(n) — two linear passes plus one sum pass
- **Space:** O(n) — three arrays (`rating`, `left`, `right`)

## Compile and Run

    gcc -std=c99 -Wall -Wextra q5.c -o q5
    ./q5

## Sample

Input:

    5
    1 2 2 3 2

Output:

    Candies: 1 2 1 2 1
    Minimum candies: 7

Walk through it:
- Left pass:  `[1, 2, 1, 2, 1]`
- Right pass: `[1, 2, 1, 2, 1]`
- max:        `[1, 2, 1, 2, 1]` → total 7
